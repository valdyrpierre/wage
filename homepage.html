Why this exists

Most hourly and commission jobs give you a paycheck every two weeks and little else — you find out what you earned after the fact, once it's too late to catch a mistake or plan around it. WageTracker flips that: you see your pay accumulate in real time, know the moment you hit overtime, and can tell instantly whether a paycheck looks right.
